Your tenant login operates one store. Confirm the store name before working. There is no cross-tenant workspace selector here. Staff roles determine which menus and actions you see; a missing action can be a permission or record-state restriction.

## Choose the task you need

| Task | Open | Check first |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare the store | My store, Settings, payment methods and shipping | Check identity, allowed methods and delivery configuration. |
| Prepare an offer | Catalog → Products, pricing and inventory | Product, variant, price, stock and publication are separate. |
| Handle a purchase | Orders, Payments and Shipments | Choose only permitted transitions after reading the record. |
| Resolve a return | Returns and Refunds | Physical receipt and financial refund are different. |
| Promote or support the store | Marketing, CMS, feedback and Support | A banner does not activate a discount. |

## Make the store ready to trade

1. Review **My store**, branches and store settings for the correct identity and operational details.
2. Check the offered currencies, tax inputs and enabled payment methods. Gateway credentials and the customer-facing method label serve different purposes.
3. Configure delivery through the available zones, methods and rates; provider setup alone does not create a usable quote.
4. Review vendor participation and staff roles before delegating work.

Open the customer website to inspect the result. Storefront branding, maintenance and CMS content affect presentation, while stock, pricing and payment/delivery eligibility govern whether a purchase can proceed.

## Prepare the catalog and availability

Use **Catalog → Products** with Categories, Brands and Attributes to describe the offer and its selectable variants. Confirm name, SKU, seller and publication state before making changes. The parent product describes the offer; the variant carries the purchasable identity.

Review **Pricing** or price-list eligibility together with currency, quantity and buyer/group. Review inventory balances and their location or history separately. Available stock differs from physical and reserved stock. A saved draft or an active-looking price record does not by itself prove the item is published and buyable. Check the final product on the customer website.

<SystemTour tour="tenant-catalog" compact />

## Work through the order desk

1. Open **Orders** and match the customer, seller, invoice and current order state. Customers place purchases; this screen manages the existing order rather than rewriting its original items.
2. Inspect **Payments** for the actual money state. For a manual transfer, read the offered proof before approving or rejecting. Record COD collection only after collection happens.
3. Open the order and choose an allowed status transition, cancellation or note according to your permissions and its current state.
4. Follow **Shipments** for parcel progress and **Returns** for eligible post-delivery work.

Payment success does not mean delivery. Return approval does not mean a refund is settled. For refunds, check the eligible amount, destination and recorded settlement state. If an action times out, read the record/history before repeating it.

<SystemTour tour="tenant-orders" compact />

## Grow the store and close the loop

Use Coupons, Promotions and Flash sales for their actual eligibility, dates, quantity limits and reward rules. Banners and CMS direct attention; they do not create a discount. Loyalty programs require their applicable rule versions; gift cards and wallet credit have their own balances and restrictions.

Review **Commissions**, **Settlements** and **Payouts** by vendor, period and currency. An approval is different from a real transfer. Follow disputed lines through the offered workflow instead of altering historical economics. Use **Support tickets**, Reviews and Questions for customer follow-up, and Reports/Audit logs/Webhooks for operational evidence. An active webhook registration does not prove delivery to the receiving system.

<SystemTour tour="tenant-growth" compact />

## When a record or action is missing

Use Tenant admins and Tenant roles for staff. Users and User groups describe customers and commercial grouping, not staff access. If a screen is unavailable, confirm your permission and store context. Escalate with the affected record and time; never include gateway credentials, passwords or unnecessary payment documents.
