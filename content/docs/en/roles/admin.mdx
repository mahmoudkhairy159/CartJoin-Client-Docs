Your platform account oversees multiple stores. Before interpreting a row, identify its tenant, vendor, currency and period. A visible screen does not grant every action: menus, related tabs and buttons depend on your permissions.

## Start with the question you need to answer

| Task | Open | Check first |
|---|---|---|
| What needs attention? | Dashboard, notifications and reports | Check the reporting period and generated time. |
| Can this vendor sell here? | Tenants, Vendors and tenant-vendor participation | Vendor identity and store-specific approval are different. |
| What happened to this purchase? | Orders → invoice → payments/refunds | Read order, money and parcel states separately. |
| What is payable? | Commissions, Settlements and Payouts | Match currency, period and state. |
| Who can do this? | Roles and Admins | Platform staff is separate from store/vendor staff. |

## Read the daily overview

Open **Dashboard**, choose Today, 7 days or 30 days, and read the generated or updated time. Summary cards and charts use the stated period; some waiting-work counts are current snapshots. Open the linked list to inspect the underlying records. A zero chart is not evidence that no store has orders.

Use **Notifications** to follow messages addressed to your account. **Reports** may require an already generated snapshot for the chosen dates. If a report is not ready, do not describe the result as zero sales. Check available export or job status before requesting another long-running operation.

## Review the business and its participation

Open **Tenants** for the store identity and **Vendors** for the global seller organization. A vendor record alone does not approve that seller in every store. Review the tenant-vendor participation record, its state and related records before deciding what it permits.

Read documents, addresses and payout accounts as separate evidence. A saved bank destination is not automatically verified, and a verified destination is not a completed payout. Follow the approval controls actually offered to your account; do not substitute a profile edit for an approval workflow.

<SystemTour tour="admin-partners" compact />

## Investigate a purchase without changing its fulfillment

1. Open **Orders** and use the tenant and available filters to find the intended purchase.
2. Open the record, match its number and read the frozen items, vendor context and status history.
3. Follow its invoice and payment records, then the shipment or return evidence when needed.
4. Give the tenant team the relevant record number and the specific discrepancy.

**Platform order oversight is read-only.** It does not create, edit, cancel or advance the order. A paid invoice does not prove delivery, and an approved return does not prove a settled refund. If an action belongs to the tenant workflow, hand it to an authorized tenant operator.

<SystemTour tour="admin-purchase" compact />

## Keep financial and access controls separate

Read **Commissions**, **Settlements** and **Payouts** in order: a calculation/ledger entry, a period statement and a transfer state answer different questions. Match the currency and period before comparing records. Never combine amounts in different currencies into a single total. Wallet credit, loyalty points and gift-card balances also have different meanings.

Use **Roles** and **Admins** for the platform team. Tenant and vendor staff use their own administration areas. Viewing, approving and exporting are separate permissions. When investigating a change, use **Audit logs** to match actor, time and affected record; an audit row proves that event, not an entire business process.

<SystemTour tour="admin-control" compact />

## When a record or action is missing

If a record or action is missing, confirm the tenant scope, filters and destination permission. Do not widen access just to make a button appear. Refresh before repeating a timed-out action and inspect its history first. Escalate with screen name, record ID and time; omit credentials and financial secrets.

## Review merchant activation and service billing

Use **Administration → Packages & versions** to create an immutable bilingual offer version, **Merchant applications** to review a submitted business, and **Subscription & setup** to select a tenant and inspect its platform billing. Confirm scope before using finance recovery. Paid activation exceptions and unpaid invoice withdrawal preserve an audit trail. [Read the billing guide](/en/features/subscriptions) and [assisted activation steps](/en/features/merchant-onboarding).
