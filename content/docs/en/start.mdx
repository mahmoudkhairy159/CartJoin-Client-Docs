## What CartJoin does

CartJoin hosts independent tenant storefronts. Vendors may sell through more than one approved tenant. Customers shop within one storefront, pay against one invoice and receive separately fulfilled vendor orders.

## Read the screen correctly

Lists use available filters and server-side paging. Open a row to inspect its facts and related records. A permission to view does not grant creation, editing, exporting or approval. A successful save is different from publication, financial settlement or completion of a queued task.

Names shown in English and Arabic may differ slightly between screens. URLs in this guide are frontend paths, not API endpoints. A vendor workspace is an active tenant choice; a tenant operator’s scope comes from their account; the platform admin’s tenant scope is an oversight filter.
