## What was checked

Marketplace operations: policy acceptance, vendor reply/appeal, store review,
platform assignment/dismissal, work queues and immutable history were checked
with synthetic local examples in EN/AR. Three contextual tours and six30–40s
captioned screenshot clips played to completion with the correct default
language and no captured console/media errors. Backend evidence hardening:
53 tests239 assertions; Order regression62 tests177 assertions. Admin55 focused
and related checks pass. These results do not complete later roadmap phases.

- Extracted frontend routes and mapped every entry to a guide or classified error/developer screen.
- Reviewed checkout/invoice/order ownership and inventory, payment, refund and selected finance constraints.
- Signed into demo accounts and observed selected screens with real screenshots.

## Limits and known gaps

- Deployed demo screens were observed without transactions. New local billing/application captures use explicitly labeled test records and simulated evidence; no real charge or transfer occurred.
- Scoped backend and dashboard tests were run for centralized identity and the new mode, subscription and application workflows. Documentation builds, link checks and browser checks are separate evidence; these checks do not certify a live deployment.
- A route is not proof of a complete feature. General Pricing and Promotion remain thin in the status index; advanced recommendations are deferred.
- Backend documentation contains stale contradictory readiness paragraphs, so this guide does not assert that every module is complete.
- The current buyer surface is invoice-based; some paths labeled orders display invoice purchases.
- Homepage copy about return days, shipping and payment options is demo content, not a universal tenant policy.
- Local source review does not establish that the deployed build matches the checkout. Screenshots reflect the observed deployment on their date.
