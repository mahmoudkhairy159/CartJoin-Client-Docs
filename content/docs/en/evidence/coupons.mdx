## Batch creation and export

The tenant application routes are `/coupons/batches` and `/coupons/batches/new`. `Modules/Coupon/routes/tenant-api.php` defines `GET /coupons/batches`, `POST /coupons/batches` and `GET /coupons/batches/{id}/export` under `/api/tenant/v1`. `CreateCouponBatchRequest` adds `batch_name`, `quantity`, `prefix` and `request_id` to the shared rules. The coupon report includes `batch_id`, `batch_name`, `batches` and the store and platform `batch_vendors` breakdown.

## Redemption evidence

Admin and Tenant history resources expose `invoice_id`, `currency_code`, `discount_amount`, `shipping_discount_amount`, `confirmed_at`, and `released_at`. The discount total includes the shipping portion. Historical redemptions can reference `order_id`.
