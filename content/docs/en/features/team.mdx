## How to use it

Open the staff list for your organization. Review roles first, create or edit the staff record, assign appropriate roles, and have the staff member sign in through the matching dashboard. Revisit access when responsibilities change.

<SystemTour tour="feature-team" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

View, create, update, remove and export are independent permissions. Granting list access does not grant every action. Assign only roles available in the current organization; vendor staff do not gain another vendor identity.

## Data flow and outcome

Organization → role permissions → staff assignment → authenticated actions. Account activity and audit evidence identify the actor who performed the operation.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
