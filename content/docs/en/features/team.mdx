## How to use it

Open the staff list for your organization. Review roles first, create or edit the staff record, assign appropriate roles, and have the staff member sign in through the matching dashboard. Revisit access when responsibilities change.

In the merchant dashboard, open **Staff invitations** from **Tenant admins**. Enter the staff member’s contact details and choose a role. The owner can select active branches to limit access from the moment the staff member accepts. Leaving the branch selection empty grants store-wide access under the selected role. The invitee sets their own password using the email link and must have an active staff seat available when they accept. Resending rotates the link. Revoking stops a pending invitation from being used.

The owner can restrict an eligible staff account to specific branches from its detail page. These assignments apply to supported branch, order and inventory workflows in addition to role permissions. To request a new owner, select one eligible staff member under **My store → Owners**, record the reason and confirm your current password. The recipient accepts from **Profile** with their own current password before the ownership changes. The requester can cancel there before acceptance.

In the vendor dashboard, open **Staff → Invitations** to send an email invitation and optionally select a role. Accepting an invitation establishes the staff account for that vendor. Workspace access follows the vendor's approved store links.

An active vendor owner with permission to update staff can open an eligible colleague and choose **Transfer ownership**. The colleague must be active and unblocked with a role that can manage staff. Confirmation transfers the requesting operator's owner status in one action. The previous owner signs in again afterward. Both vendor and platform staff lists offer queued exports where the role allows them.

## Restrictions and decisions

View, create, update, remove and export are independent permissions. Assign roles from the current organization. Vendor staff cannot assign themselves a different role, and a delegated operator cannot grant permissions beyond their own. Editing a role affects all staff assigned to it. Reassign staff before deleting a role. The vendor must retain an eligible owner.

## Data flow and outcome

Organization → role permissions → staff assignment → authenticated actions. Account activity and audit evidence identify the actor who performed the operation.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
