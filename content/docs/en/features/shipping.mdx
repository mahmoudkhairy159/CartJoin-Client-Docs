## How to use it

Tenant operators configure zones, methods and rates, and inspect rate quotes. For fulfillment, open Shipments, select the permitted order/items, and follow picking, packing and dispatch actions. Customers track their parcels from account or the tracking surface.

<SystemTour tour="feature-shipping" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

A shipping quote is not a dispatched shipment. Normal shipment states progress pending → picked → packed → dispatched → in transit → delivered or returned. Rates and eligible destinations must match current configuration; do not override a failed quote with an invented cost.

## Address coverage and method cost

Delivery options are resolved from the selected country, state and city, including saved addresses represented by location IDs. A method has its own identity, name, charge and estimated delivery days. Checkout uses the chosen method’s charge. A delivery estimate is not a dispatched parcel or a guaranteed arrival time.

## Data flow and outcome

Address + zone/method/rate → checkout shipping amount → shipment and events → delivery signal → order/post-delivery services.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

## Labels and customer privacy

A label records a carrier reference, not dispatch or delivery. [Follow the tenant/vendor label guide](/en/features/shipping-providers#carrier-label-screen-guide). Customer tracking checks both the signed-in buyer and current store, including the alternate carrier tracking endpoint. A global customer account cannot open another buyer’s parcel or use its signup-origin store to bypass shipment ownership.


For item quantities, confirmation and receipt history, use [Packing and parcels](/en/features/packing).
