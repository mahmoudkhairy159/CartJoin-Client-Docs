## How to use it

Open Audit logs, filter by the available actor, event or record fields and inspect the entry alongside the actual business record and its own history. Record the relevant identifiers when escalating a discrepancy.

<SystemTour tour="feature-audit" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

An audit entry is evidence, not a control to undo the action. Investigations must respect tenant and vendor boundaries. Do not expose secrets or unnecessary personal data in exported evidence.

## Data flow and outcome

Authorized mutation → actor/event metadata → audit record → scoped search and investigation.

The queued audit CSV requires both audit export and report export permission. Choose a UTC date range and follow the export in **Export history**. Large result sets must be split into smaller date ranges. A queued export and its download are recorded in the audit log.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
