## How to use it

Operators open Users to locate the customer and related purchases. Customers use Account → Profile to maintain personal details and saved addresses. Operators use User groups and Price lists together when configuring group eligibility.

## Registration store is fixed

When a platform admin edits an existing customer, the Tenant picker is disabled. It retains the store recorded when the customer was created. A reassignment request is rejected with a field validation error. Create a customer in the intended store initially; do not move their account to change an order's store.

<SystemScreenshot src="/docs/screenshots/admin-customer-signup-origin-en.webp" alt="Platform-admin customer edit: the registration store is locked and explained below the field." />

## One account across stores

Use the same CartJoin customer account and password at another store. You do not register again. Each store keeps its own basket, purchases, addresses, default delivery address, loyalty and balances. Signing in creates the private store relationship automatically; it never changes the first-registration store.

<SystemScreenshot src="/docs/screenshots/customer-central-signin-en.webp" alt="Customer sign-in explains the shared account and private store purchases." />

## Manage access to your store

Tenant operators open **Users → customer → Edit**. Identity fields are read-only. Change **Active** or **Blocked** to control this store only. **Revoke store access** preserves the central account, other stores and purchase history. Only the customer manages their global profile/password through the authorized account and recovery screens. A platform-wide security block still applies everywhere.

<SystemScreenshot src="/docs/screenshots/tenant-central-customer-access-en.webp" alt="Tenant form locks central identity fields and allows local access controls." />

New merchant-created customers must verify ownership through password recovery before choosing a password. The merchant cannot choose their password. Tenant customer identity imports are unavailable; authorized platform imports remain separate. Tenant lists, groups and exports use store relationships. Signup-origin statistics remain separate from customers acquired by later stores.

## Restrictions and decisions

Customers see their own account records only. Saved address changes do not rewrite the delivery address snapshot of an existing order. A group membership does not grant dashboard staff permissions.

## Using the address book

The address form loads the country list and sorts location names alphabetically. Pick a country to load its states, then a state to load its cities. The two street lines are stored together as one address, and saved street text is reused at checkout. Check the recipient details shown in checkout; an address without its own recipient name can use the account holder’s name.

## Data flow and outcome

Customer → addresses and groups → cart eligibility → invoice and order snapshots. Historical purchases preserve the values used at checkout.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
