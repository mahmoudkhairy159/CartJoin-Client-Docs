## How to use it

Operators open Users to locate the customer and related purchases. Customers use Account → Profile to maintain personal details and saved addresses. Operators use User groups and Price lists together when configuring group eligibility.

<SystemTour tour="feature-customers" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Customers see their own account records only. Saved address changes do not rewrite the delivery address snapshot of an existing order. A group membership does not grant dashboard staff permissions.

## Using the address book

The address form loads the country list and sorts location names alphabetically. Pick a country to load its states, then a state to load its cities. The two street lines are stored together as one address, and saved street text is reused at checkout. Check the recipient details shown in checkout; an address without its own recipient name can use the account holder’s name.

## Data flow and outcome

Customer → addresses and groups → cart eligibility → invoice and order snapshots. Historical purchases preserve the values used at checkout.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
