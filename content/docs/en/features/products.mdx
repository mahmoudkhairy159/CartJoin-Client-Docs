## How to use it

Create or open a product. Maintain translations, category, brand and images. Configure simple or variable options and active variants, check prices and stock, then use the available publish or submit-for-review action. Confirm visibility in the storefront.

For a new external-vendor activation or republication, the vendor must accept
the current store service policy and supply its required approved, valid
documents. A blocked attempt explains the missing requirement and keeps the
draft unpublished. Existing offer corrections, stock, drafts and unpublishing
remain available. [Read the policy and evidence workflow](/en/features/marketplace-operations).

[Follow the vendor submission, correction and publication workflow](/en/features/product-review).

## Restrictions and decisions

A visible product needs active publication and an active positively priced variant under the publication rules. Cart lines reference variants. Variant and image IDs must belong to the selected product. Vendors manage only their own tenant-workspace products.

## Data flow and outcome

Product + variants + images → publication → search/catalog → cart variant → immutable order item snapshot. Later catalog edits do not alter old purchase prices.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
