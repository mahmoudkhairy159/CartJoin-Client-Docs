## How to use it

Platform admins open Tenants to inspect or manage the organization. Tenant operators use My store and Tenant branches to maintain their own information and locations. Confirm the store name and address before saving.

<SystemTour tour="feature-tenants" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Tenant operators cannot select a different tenant by editing a form. Store identity, branch records and catalog must stay in the resolved tenant. A branch is not automatically an inventory location or a shipping zone.

## Data flow and outcome

Tenant → staff, branches, settings and catalog. Domain/context resolution chooses which storefront data a customer sees.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
