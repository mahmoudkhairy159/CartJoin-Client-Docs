## How to use it

Platform admins open Tenants to inspect or manage the organization. Tenant operators use My store and Tenant branches to maintain their own information and locations. Confirm the store name and address before saving.

<SystemTour tour="feature-tenants" compact />

## Operate a branch

A new branch starts in **Draft**. Open **Branch operations** to activate it, select a default branch, and set opening hours or pickup availability. Active branch capacity is subject to the store’s package. Customers see published branches in the storefront directory. Inventory locations can reference a branch, while stock and work in progress retain their own records.

Set a replacement before closing a default branch. Use **Closing** to wind down operations. Before archiving, resolve open orders, stock balances and active reservations. Permanent deletion is available only when the removal preview finds no dependent records and the branch is in an eligible state.

## Connect a storefront domain

In **Storefront settings → Storefront domains**, the owner adds a hostname and places the displayed TXT record at their DNS provider. After verification the owner can make it primary or revoke it. See that routing and HTTPS are configured before sharing the address with customers.

## Suspend, reactivate or archive a store

Authorized platform operators enter a reason to suspend or reactivate a store. Suspension revokes tenant staff sessions and blocks new sales. Reactivation restores eligible store access under the current billing and selling rules. Archive a closed merchant to preserve its records. Permanent deletion is limited to eligible inactive stores without retained dependencies.

When enabled in a sandbox, authorized platform staff can open a **Sandbox support view** from a store's details. Enter a reason to view tenant status, launch status and counts for active and total branches and staff. The session expires after 15 minutes, and you can end it sooner. CartJoin records who opened, viewed and ended each session.

## Restrictions and decisions

Tenant operators cannot select a different tenant by editing a form. Store identity, branch records and catalog must stay in the resolved tenant. A branch is not automatically an inventory location or a shipping zone.

## Data flow and outcome

Tenant → staff, branches, settings and catalog. Domain/context resolution chooses which storefront data a customer sees.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
