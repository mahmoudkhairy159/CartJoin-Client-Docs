## How to use it

Customers use product feedback controls and Account → Reviews. Tenant operators use Reviews, Questions and Review settings to inspect and moderate. Vendors review or answer feedback only where their own-product permissions allow it.

<SystemTour tour="feature-reviews" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Submission does not guarantee immediate publication. Ownership, purchase eligibility and moderation policy determine allowed actions. Replying to feedback does not authorize editing the original purchase.

## Data flow and outcome

Customer submission → eligibility/moderation → public review or answer → catalog trust signals and notifications.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
