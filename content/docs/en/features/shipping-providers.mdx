## How to use it

Open Shipping providers on an authorized dashboard. Review carrier availability and configured account/settings, then use the shipment’s offered dispatch/tracking controls. Investigate failed provider calls before retrying a physical dispatch.

<SystemTour tour="feature-shipping-providers" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Provider credentials are configuration secrets and do not belong in customer guides or screenshots. A carrier acceptance event is different from proof of delivery. Platform visibility does not imply tenant carrier configuration rights.

## Data flow and outcome

Configured carrier → dispatch request → tracking reference/events → shipment status and customer tracking.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
