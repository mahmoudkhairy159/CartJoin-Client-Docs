## How to use it

Open Shipping providers on an authorized dashboard. Review carrier availability and configured account/settings, then use the shipment’s offered dispatch/tracking controls. Investigate failed provider calls before retrying a physical dispatch.

On a shipment, open shipping costs to compare saved carrier quotes with the buyer’s delivery charge. For Bosta, operators with COD reconciliation access can also review allocated statement fees and adjustments there. The Bosta booking panel explains why a parcel is ineligible, including COD scope or missing credentials. A quote can be recorded for a carrier whose booking and labels you manage outside CartJoin.

## Restrictions and decisions

Provider credentials are configuration secrets and do not belong in customer guides or screenshots. A carrier acceptance event is different from proof of delivery. Platform visibility does not imply tenant carrier configuration rights.

## Data flow and outcome

Configured carrier → dispatch request → tracking reference/events → shipment status and customer tracking.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

## Test carrier and uncertain bookings

The development test carrier creates private PDFs marked **TEST / NOT VALID FOR SHIPPING**. It makes no real booking or charge. Its signed callbacks retain durable replay evidence; old progress does not move a delivered shipment backwards. Manual providers reject external callbacks and use authorized staff progress updates.

If an adapter times out, inspect the carrier booking before repeating a purchase. The system asks for review rather than claiming a successful manual fallback. A provider with retained tracking evidence is deactivated instead of deleting that history. Live carrier activation requires separate provider access and verification.


## Create and review a shipping label

1. **Tenant operator:** open **Shipping → Carriers**. The **Vendor labels** column controls new vendor label requests for each connected carrier. Use its Allow/Block action only with the dedicated permission, and read the confirmation. Existing labels and history remain available for authorized servicing.
2. Open the relevant **Shipment → Labels**. Choose a carrier by name. A manual label requires its PDF, ZPL or image file; a generated label does not require a placeholder upload.
3. **Vendor:** select the approved store workspace, open **Shipping → your shipment → Add label**, then choose an offered carrier. An empty list means the store has no eligible carrier enabled for your use; ask its operator.
4. Review the saved tracking number and **Open/Download** link. Generating the same shipment/carrier label again reuses its saved result. Creating a different label after dispatch is refused. A generated tracking reference does not mark the parcel shipped or delivered.

The test option exists only in enabled local/test environments and is permanently marked **TEST — not valid for shipping**. Its download is private and temporary; reload the shipment to obtain a fresh link after expiry. No customer address, real booking or payment is sent to a carrier. A named carrier in the catalog is not proof that a live adapter is connected.

If generation times out, investigate the carrier result before trying again. Removing a label row does not cancel a carrier booking; retained signed tracking evidence can block deletion. Disabling vendor label access affects new requests, while approved vendors retain access to their authorized historical shipment records.
