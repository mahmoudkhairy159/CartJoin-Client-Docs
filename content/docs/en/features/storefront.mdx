Language and currency changes preserve the current page and form inputs. Catalog prices identify the selected display currency and use the configured exchange rates. The cart and invoices display their own transaction currency.

## How to use it

Tenant operators open Storefront, My store and Settings for their respective controls. Review branding, visible content and operational options, then inspect the customer site. Use platform Storefront settings only for the controls explicitly exposed there.

In **Storefront → Design**, choose a template and enter its English or Arabic heading and introduction. You can also edit homepage editorial copy, choose which sections appear, and set the browser icon and share image. Save and preview the draft on the storefront, then publish it. Clear a field or select **Inherit** to restore the corresponding default after publication. Catalog names and the details on invoices still come from the store's records.

In **Storefront → Settings**, choose where the announcement bar appears and
set an optional expiry in your browser's time zone. Leave page selection empty
to show it on all supported pages. Leave expiry blank to keep it running until
you disable it. The strip hides at expiry even if the shopper leaves the page
open.

## Restrictions and decisions

The storefront domain/context selects the tenant; visual branding does not change ownership. Maintenance mode can prevent normal shopping. Configuration labels may differ across the tenant and platform dashboards.

## Publication and currency controls

Publishing a product refreshes the storefront’s cached catalog views. Review the published storefront after an authorized catalog change. The footer places common currencies first and offers More/Less for the remaining choices; selecting a display preference does not bypass the store’s checkout-currency rules.

## Data flow and outcome

Resolved tenant → published store configuration + content + catalog → customer site and maintenance behavior.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
