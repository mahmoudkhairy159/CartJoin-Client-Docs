## How to use it

Tenant operators open Storefront, My store and Settings for their respective controls. Review branding, visible content and operational options, then inspect the customer site. Use platform Storefront settings only for the controls explicitly exposed there.

In **Storefront → Design**, choose a template and enter its English or Arabic heading and introduction. You can also edit homepage editorial copy, choose which sections appear, and set the browser icon and share image. Save and preview the draft on the storefront, then publish it. Clear a field or select **Inherit** to restore the corresponding default after publication. Catalog names and the details on invoices still come from the store's records.

In **Storefront → Home layout**, arrange banners and product sections. Add localized product section headings where needed. An empty localized heading uses the shared heading, and an empty published layout uses the standard homepage. Save the draft, preview it in English or Arabic in a desktop or mobile-sized window, then publish it to show the layout to shoppers. You can open the private draft preview during maintenance. Content from public catalog requests may be unavailable in the preview while maintenance is active.

**Publication history** in Design and Home layout lists earlier live revisions. Choose **Use in draft** to restore the part of a prior publication managed on that page. Review the restored draft and publish it when ready. If someone changed the draft first, review your current edits before loading the latest revision.

In **Storefront → Settings**, choose where the announcement bar appears and
set an optional expiry in your browser's time zone. Leave page selection empty
to show it on all supported pages. Leave expiry blank to keep it running until
you disable it. The strip hides at expiry even if the shopper leaves the page
open.

To publish separate English and Arabic announcement copy, fill in **Announcement translations** under Design. A blank translation uses the base announcement message from Storefront settings. Page targeting, expiry, and the enable switch are managed in Storefront settings.

Save footer copyright and social links under **Storefront → Settings**. These values enter the design draft. Open **Storefront → Design** to preview and publish them. Publication history can restore the previous footer and links into a new draft.

## Continue an existing purchase

Sign in on the owning store to open your invoices, resume payment, track parcels, or start a return. Purchase history, account credit, and loyalty are scoped to the signed-in customer and this store.

## Restrictions and decisions

The storefront domain determines the tenant. In **Storefront → Settings**, authorized tenant staff can switch on maintenance mode to pause shopping and new purchases. Existing order tracking, invoices, and payments remain available.

## Publication and currency controls

Publishing a product refreshes the storefront’s cached catalog views. Review the published storefront after an authorized catalog change. The footer places common currencies first and offers More/Less for the remaining choices; selecting a display preference does not bypass the store’s checkout-currency rules.

## Data flow and outcome

Resolved tenant → published store configuration + content + catalog → customer site and maintenance behavior.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

## Guest checkout update

Manage guest checkout in **Storefront → Settings**. When enabled, guests can use a basket before sign-in and make eligible cash-on-delivery purchases. A phone number is required and email is optional. Guests can still access receipts for earlier purchases after you turn off guest checkout. [Read the complete guest checkout and phone-account guide](/en/workflows/guest-checkout).
