## How to use it

Tenant operators open Refunds, inspect the order and available amounts, preview the selected quantities/value, then initiate through the authorized flow. Follow provider completion or settle/withdraw manual-pending refunds with evidence. Vendors inspect their own refund share.

Vendor staff can inspect refund information. Authorized administrators can close the order refund once all successful refunds for that order cover its full total. A partial refund keeps the order open.

## Restrictions and decisions

The ceiling is the smaller of the order’s remaining refundable value and shared payment headroom. Pending refunds reserve value. Duplicate line IDs are invalid, quantities cap value, and a sibling vendor order’s funds cannot cover an over-refund. Vendor access is read-only.

## Data flow and outcome

Eligible order lines → preview allocation → refund command → online confirmation or manual settlement → refund ledger, payment totals and credit note. Partial refunds across siblings do not mark the whole shared payment fully refunded.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
