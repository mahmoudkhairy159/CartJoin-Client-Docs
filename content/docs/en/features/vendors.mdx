## How to use it

Platform staff create the vendor organization, its staff account and its tenant relationship. Review the vendor record and supporting documents under **Vendors**. The store or platform can approve a link when the vendor is active and has an active, unblocked owner whose role can manage staff. The vendor then signs in, selects an approved workspace and reviews its terms.

## Document expiry and platform review

**Vendor:** open **My store → Documents**. Read verification status and **Expiry date** separately. Filter this vendor's list by expired, next-30-day or undated records. Choose **Upload document**, select its type and enter the printed date using the calendar. Leave the date blank when unknown or inapplicable. A vendor upload always starts **Pending review**.

**Platform reviewer:** open **Vendors → selected vendor → Compliance → Documents**. Expired evidence cannot be approved, including through generic create/edit requests. Approve and reject require their own permissions; upload or edit permission alone is insufficient.

The edit form starts with **No explicit status change**. Changing the file, type or expiry—including clearing a date—returns the document to Pending unless an authorized reviewer explicitly chooses a reviewed status. Leaving the file empty preserves the stored file. A document cannot be reassigned to another vendor; create a separate evidence record instead.

A date remains current through the end of its UTC calendar day. The warning window includes today and the next 30 days. **No expiry recorded** means no date was supplied, not permanent validity. Expiry does not automatically suspend a vendor in every marketplace. Workspace participation, payout verification and document verification remain separate controls.

These screenshots use synthetic local expiry metadata. Some preview rows deliberately have no compliance file; the upload form was demonstrated without sending one. Source tests verify review rules separately. Tenant operators do not gain access to global compliance files. The tested development build now includes [service policies, cases, replies, appeals and operational queues](/en/features/marketplace-operations); rejection receipts, publisher permissions and carrier-to-order lifecycle integration remain tracked work.

## Restrictions and decisions

Each store approves its own vendor link independently. Suspending a link removes that workspace and its offers. Disabling the vendor organization ends sign-in and catalog eligibility in every workspace. Tenant or platform staff can still service existing orders and recorded payouts using their own permissions. Vendors with tenant links or commerce history cannot be permanently deleted.

## Find the seller before choosing a product

Open **Vendors** and search by name. Select a seller to read its description, public office or returns addresses and eligible products. The directory lists active sellers approved for the current store when marketplace participation is enabled and the storefront is open. Guests can browse and may use [guest checkout](/en/workflows/guest-checkout) where available. Check delivery options during checkout.

## Data flow and outcome

Vendor identity + tenant approval → selectable workspace → vendor-owned products and orders. Approval evidence is distinct from product moderation.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

### Apply to become a seller

Open **Sell on CartJoin** in Platform Discovery, enter the organization and owner details, and request a verification code. Enter the emailed code and choose a password. The platform team reviews verified applications and notifies the applicant of the decision. Approval creates the vendor organization and its owner account. A store operator must approve a separate tenant link before the seller can work in that store.

### Fulfillment and sponsored placements

A tenant operator configures branch permissions, order acceptance windows and delivery areas on the linked seller's detail page. With no branch assignments, the store default applies. With assignments, checkout uses the highest-priority active location that can fulfill for that seller, and seller inventory adjustments require a branch that permits stocking. Windows use the configured time zone, support closing after midnight, and are enforced when quoting and placing an order. Delivery areas narrow the destinations allowed by the store. Customers can see the schedule and configured areas on the seller page.

A tenant operator books one fixed-price home slot against a published offer. A different authorized staff member verifies payment received outside CartJoin. The slot is exclusive during its booked window and displays a Sponsored label while the offer remains eligible and stocked. Cancellation removes the placement from the storefront. Payment collection and any reimbursement are handled outside CartJoin.
