## How to use it

Review the vendor organization and supporting documents. Open Tenant vendors to inspect the relationship and its approval state. Vendor users sign in, then choose an approved workspace and review Workspace terms before operating.

## Restrictions and decisions

An active vendor organization alone does not approve every tenant relationship. Every vendor record request is limited by vendor identity and active tenant. A workspace switch must refresh the displayed data.

## Find the seller before choosing a product

Open Vendors and search by name. Select a seller to read its description, available public addresses and product collection. A seller without a dedicated page can open a seller-filtered Shop view. You can browse these pages as a guest; saving or buying requires your customer account. The displayed directory count is not a delivery guarantee.

## Data flow and outcome

Vendor identity + tenant approval → selectable workspace → vendor-owned products and orders. Approval evidence is distinct from product moderation.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
