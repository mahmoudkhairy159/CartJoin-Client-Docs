## How to use it

Use Shop filters for category, brand, vendor and price. Open a product to choose a variant. Operators review Search query log for poor results and use Recommendations where exposed to review configured suggestions.

<SystemTour tour="feature-search" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Search must stay in the storefront tenant and must not expose inactive offers. Recommendation routes exist while advanced roadmap behavior remains deferred in the status matrix. An empty result may reflect filters or publication, not missing catalog data.

## Data flow and outcome

Published catalog → indexed/discovery results → product page → cart. Query logs are operational evidence, not completed purchases.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
