## How to use it

Select Today, 7 days or 30 days where offered. Check the generated/updated time and use Refresh when needed. Follow attention links to failed payments, pending approvals, open orders or low stock, then inspect the records before acting.

## Restrictions and decisions

Period metrics and current snapshots may appear together. Zero activity in the selected period does not mean there are no historical orders. The admin dashboard can span tenants; the vendor dashboard remains tied to its active workspace.

## Data flow and outcome

Scoped transaction data → cached/generated aggregates → cards/charts → filtered operational detail. Always reconcile financial decisions with the underlying ledger.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
