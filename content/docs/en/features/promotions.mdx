## How to use it

Choose a draft promotion and add a rule with type `product` and payload `{"product_ids":[12,24]}`. Add a `percentage` reward with `{"value":"10.00"}` for ten percent off, or choose `fixed` with `{"amount":"25.00","currency":"EGP"}`. Activate the draft and check a matching product in the cart. Cart thresholds, category conditions and free shipping currently have no effect on line prices.

## Restrictions and decisions

Do not assume automatic stacking, advanced campaign scheduling or every planned rule is implemented. A route alone proves navigation, not the depth of the promotion engine. Published offers must agree with server-calculated totals.

## Data flow and outcome

Configured promotion input → available pricing integration → cart evaluation. Treat unsupported roadmap strategies as unavailable until verified.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
