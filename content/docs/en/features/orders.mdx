From a delivered order in your account, choose **Buy again**. The store checks the original variants against current catalog availability, stock and prices. A repeat request keeps the larger quantity already in your cart. If any line is unavailable, your cart stays as it was.

## How to use it

Open Orders, filter by the available status or ownership fields, and open the detail. Inspect items, payment state and history. Tenant operators use allowed status changes and notes. Vendors advance their own fulfillment only through offered transitions.

A payment settlement review badge means a captured payment needs attention before fulfillment. Check stock for this vendor order. Once the missing stock is available, the maintenance worker can retry settlement. A vendor can continue shipping after the hold clears.

Tenant staff with permission to view orders receive an inbox notice when an order first goes on hold. A cancellation or refund after capture needs manual reconciliation before fulfillment.

## Restrictions and decisions

Platform order screens are oversight only. Normal progress is pending → confirmed → processing → shipped → delivered. Vendors cannot cancel from processing; tenant operators have a wider pre-handoff cancellation rule. Any retained parcel handoff blocks cancellation, even before the whole order is Shipped. A status is not a free-form field and a refund is not a total edit.

## Item snapshots and order notes

New checkout snapshots use the variant picture, then the product picture when needed. Historical orders retain their saved item identity and amounts. Platform order detail separates the notes history from the older single note field, so use the recorded note entries when following a discussion.

## Data flow and outcome

Invoice → vendor order → status history and notes → shipment/delivery → downstream commission, loyalty or return effects. Order and payment status are separate facts.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

## Follow each seller's delivery progress

Open your invoice in the current store. Each seller order has a **Delivery
progress** panel with its remaining required units and dispatched/received counts.
The purchased quantities stay visible; only settled physical-unit refunds reduce
the remaining target. A monetary refund alone does not remove a physical unit. If a later unit refund invalidates the saved parcel plan, dispatch and delivery counts show as unknown rather than zero. The verification notice does not erase earlier delivery evidence; check the order status or contact the store.

A parcel can arrive while another is still waiting. Read the per-parcel outcome
before assuming the whole order is complete. The test marker identifies a preview,
not a real delivery. Actual carrier handoff time remains unknown when it was not
provided; the screen never substitutes the time a later update was recorded.

Payment remains separate: delivery does not mean COD was collected. Once there is
retained handoff for any parcel, **Cancel order** is no longer offered and new
payment attempts are blocked; contact the store to reconcile the existing order.
If progress cannot load, choose **Try again**. Your global customer login works
across stores, while these invoices and parcel details remain private to the
store and purchase owner. Internal evidence IDs and reviewer notes are not shown.
