## How to use it

Customers open Account → Support, describe the issue and attach only relevant evidence. Operators open Support tickets, read the history, respond and follow the offered status changes. Maintain FAQ categories and translated answers for recurring questions.

<SystemTour tour="feature-support" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

A ticket is not an automatic refund, cancellation or payout instruction. Those require their own authorized workflows. Avoid placing sensitive payment details in a public FAQ or unneeded attachments.

## Read the help centre before opening a ticket

Open Help centre from the footer. Choose a category and expand a question to read its answer; this browsing does not require sign-in. If the answer does not resolve your case, use Open a ticket and sign in. In your account, include the invoice or order number, describe what happened and attach only relevant evidence. A ticket does not cancel an order or start a refund by itself.

## Data flow and outcome

Issue + customer context → ticket/messages/attachments → authorized response → resolution evidence; recurring guidance → FAQ publication.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
