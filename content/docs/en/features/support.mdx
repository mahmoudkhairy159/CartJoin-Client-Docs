## How to use it

Customers open Account → Support, select the request type or linked order when available, describe the issue, and attach relevant evidence. Keep the case number for follow-up. In the ticket, customers can reply, close a resolved case, or reopen it for review. A follow-up to a closed case starts a linked ticket.

Operators open Support tickets and check new, unassigned, and overdue cases. Assign work to an eligible operator, reply to the customer, and use an internal note for staff-only context. Choose the status that reflects who needs to act next. Escalate cases that need platform attention.

## Restrictions and decisions

A ticket is not an automatic refund, cancellation or payout instruction. Those require their own authorized workflows. Avoid placing sensitive payment details in a public FAQ or unneeded attachments.

## Read the help centre before opening a ticket

Open Help centre from the footer. Guests can browse all questions, search in the current language, choose a topic, and open an article to share its link or leave helpfulness feedback. The help centre links to order tracking, returns, and signed-in support. Attach only evidence that helps staff understand the case.

## Data flow and outcome

A submission creates a case in the store’s support queue. Replies, attachments, assignments, and status changes stay with that case. The notification inbox receives relevant changes. Submitted and Reviewing need staff attention, Waiting on customer needs a shopper reply, and Resolved, Rejected, and Closed record different outcomes.

For recurring questions, create a category and enter the question and answer in English and Arabic. Preview both languages before publishing. The storefront reads published questions under published categories. General holds published FAQs without a selected category. Reassign or remove linked FAQs before deleting their category.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
