## How to use it

Operators review Taxes and permitted currency/reference settings before publishing prices. Inspect subtotal, discounts, tax, shipping, credit and remaining due separately. Customers review the selected currency and final invoice amounts before payment.

<SystemTour tour="feature-tax-currency" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Do not add amounts in different currencies. A currency display choice does not establish that a gateway supports that currency. Money is calculated server-side with decimal arithmetic; do not recompute settlement or refund values from rounded screen labels.

## Data flow and outcome

Tenant currency/tax policy → price calculation → cart totals → immutable invoice/order tax and currency snapshots → finance reporting.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
