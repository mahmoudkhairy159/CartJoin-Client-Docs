## How to use it

Open Flash sales, maintain the campaign period and included items, then review activation and stock indicators. Customers browse Flash and open the offer’s product before selecting the variant and checking the cart price.

<SystemTour tour="feature-flash-sales" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

A countdown is not a stock reservation. Date, active state and remaining allocation must still qualify at purchase time. A displayed sale card is not proof that a stale cart retains the earlier price.

## Data flow and outcome

Campaign + included variants → eligible sale price → cart/checkout validation → allocated usage.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
