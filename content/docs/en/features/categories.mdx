## How to use it

Open Categories, inspect the tree, choose the correct parent, maintain translated names and slugs, then associate products. Review the resulting storefront navigation and category filters.

<SystemTour tour="feature-categories" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Categories belong to a tenant. A vendor may have reference access without category-management rights. Review child categories and product dependencies before removal; do not assume a delete cascades safely.

## Data flow and outcome

Category tree → product association → storefront filters → category-targeted promotion eligibility.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
