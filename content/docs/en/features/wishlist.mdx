## How to use it

Sign in, use the Wishlist control on a product, and open Account → Wishlist. Reopen the product, choose an available variant, then add it to the cart when ready.

## Restrictions and decisions

The store's feature policy determines whether you can save more products. Account → Wishlist shows when additions are unavailable. You can still open and remove items already saved in that store.

Wishlist entries belong to the customer and tenant. Saved products can later become unavailable or change price. Saving is not checkout and creates no invoice.

## Recognize the saved variant

Saved cards can include the product name, link, vendor and an image that falls back from the variant to the product. The saved item is still a variant: recheck its availability and current price before adding it to the basket.

## Keep the saved list up to date

Opening Wishlist refreshes your saved variants from the server. Saving or removing a heart also re-fetches the list. Use the product link to inspect the exact item; Move to cart is offered for purchase preparation and Remove deletes the saved entry. Check the current price and availability before buying. A saved variant does not reserve stock, and a badge alone is not the full list.

## Data flow and outcome

Customer + product reference → saved list → fresh product check → optional cart line.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
