## How to use it

Open Brands, maintain the brand name, translated content and media where offered, and associate the brand in the product editor. Customers can browse Brands and related catalog results.

<SystemTour tour="feature-brands" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

A brand is not a vendor account. Brand association does not authorize editing another vendor’s product. Tenant-scoped identifiers must be used inside the current catalog.

## Browse by brand as a customer

You can open Brands without signing in. Choose an available A–Z letter to narrow the tiles; choose it again or Clear to show all loaded brands. Open a tile to see Shop filtered to that brand. Disabled letters have no matching brand in the loaded list. A brand describes the label; the vendor is the business selling and shipping the item.

## Data flow and outcome

Brand metadata → product reference → catalog filter and brand presentation.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
