## How to use it

Open My store / Vendor profile. Review identity, addresses, documents and payout accounts in their respective sections. Submit accurate details and follow the verification state; inspect the masked destination before a settlement is approved.

<SystemTour tour="feature-vendor-profile" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

A payout account being saved does not mean it is verified. Settlement approval requires the correct tenant and vendor destination. Later account edits do not redirect a payout that already captured its destination snapshot.

## Data flow and outcome

Business details → verification → eligible payout account → payout destination snapshot. Documents and financial identifiers should be shared only with authorized reviewers.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.

## Expiry is separate from approval

The Documents tab offers a printed expiry date and expiry filters. Upload current evidence for review; an earlier approval does not override expiry. [Follow the document expiry and review walkthrough](/en/features/vendors#document-expiry-screen-guide).
