## How to use it

Open SEO, choose the relevant page/resource and maintain the available titles, descriptions and metadata. Check the public destination and locale before considering the change complete.

<SystemTour tour="feature-seo" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Metadata does not publish an otherwise hidden product or guarantee a search ranking. Slugs and destinations must remain tenant-correct. Avoid describing content that is not actually present.

## Data flow and outcome

Resource + locale → SEO metadata → rendered page/preview and indexing outputs where configured.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
