## How to use it

Open Inventory and select the variant and location. Review on-hand, reserved and available quantities. Use the authorized adjustment or transfer flow with an accurate reason; inspect the movement ledger afterward. Follow low-stock views for replenishment.

## Restrictions and decisions

Available = on hand − reserved. Reservations require enough available units. Transfers require a positive quantity and different source/destination locations. Adding to a cart is not a purchase reservation; final checkout can still reject insufficient stock.

## Data flow and outcome

Balance → checkout reservation → confirm or release → stock movement. A permitted return may add inspected units back through a return movement.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
