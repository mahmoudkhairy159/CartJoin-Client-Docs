## How to use it

Sign in, choose a variant and add it. Open Cart to adjust quantities, remove unavailable items, apply an eligible coupon and inspect credit/points options. Review refreshed totals and validation messages before checkout.

## Restrictions and decisions

No guest cart is implemented. One cart cannot mix tenants. The default distinct-line cap is 50 and configured expiry is normally seven days. Prices recalculate on mutations; stock can change before checkout. Expired carts may be cleared.

## Read quantities and expiry

Changing quantity must change the line amount, not just the displayed count. Review the refreshed totals before checkout. The storefront now shows cart expiry as a readable date and time. Product pictures use the product image when a variant has no separate picture. A cart picture or price is still provisional until purchase placement succeeds.

## Recheck the basket when you return

Opening Cart now requests the latest basket from the server. Read the variant, quantity and seller group, then the coupon, gift-card, wallet and points adjustments actually applied. An option may be absent when no eligible value is available. Delivery progress messages are estimates; use the final checkout quote. If the page cannot refresh, do not assume an older total is still valid.

## Data flow and outcome

Authenticated buyer + tenant → variant lines → recalculation and eligibility → validated checkout input. Cart totals are provisional until checkout succeeds.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
