## How to use it

Tenant operators review Gift cards and Gift-card policies before issuing or managing value. Customers use Account → Gift cards and the available redemption controls, then inspect the resulting applied amount and remaining balance.

<SystemTour tour="feature-gift-cards" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

Gift-card value is not cash in a wallet account. Eligibility, currency, state and policy determine use. Never expose a redeemable secret code in screenshots or treat a saved code as a guaranteed successful redemption.

## Claim feedback

A code that is unknown, not claimable or belongs to another customer produces a field validation message. Correct the code or contact support with a masked reference; do not repeatedly submit it or share the complete secret. A successful claim still needs the resulting balance and transaction evidence to confirm its effect.

## Data flow and outcome

Issued card → policy and balance check → reservation/redemption → order tender allocation and transaction evidence.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
