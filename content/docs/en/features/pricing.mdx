## How to use it

Review variant base prices, then tenant Product prices, Price lists and User groups where enabled. Configure the relevant list and eligibility, then review the resulting cart total for the intended buyer.

<SystemTour tour="feature-pricing" compact />

## Restrictions and decisions

The server recalculates prices. Typed browser totals are not authoritative. Existing orders keep their original money snapshots. Price maintenance requires the relevant permission and the resolved tenant workspace.

## Quantity and pricing authority

Review both the unit price and the quantity-adjusted line total. The pricing pipeline calculates line amounts from unit price × quantity; flash-sale savings apply to the same line once. Authorized tenant operators can inspect and maintain both tenant-owned and vendor-set prices within their own catalog. Vendor access remains limited to that vendor and the selected tenant workspace.

## Data flow and outcome

Variant price + eligible list + applicable discount rules → cart recalculation → checkout money snapshot.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
