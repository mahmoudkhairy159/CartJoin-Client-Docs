## How to use it

Store staff open **Price lists** to configure the audience, currency, dates, and variant prices. Choose a customer group for private prices and add its members in **User groups**. Choose the store-wide audience only for prices intended for all shoppers, including guests. Check the resulting cart for the intended buyer and quantity.

Platform staff can inspect price lists and their items from the Admin Dashboard. Store staff make pricing and membership changes from the Tenant Dashboard.

## Drafts, publishing, and CSV

Open **Drafts and versions** from a price list to prepare changes while shoppers continue to see the current prices. Set a unit price for each quantity break you want to offer. For example, a SKU can have one price at 6 units and a lower price at 12 units. A quantity break changes the unit price once the buyer reaches that quantity.

Use **Preview as customer** to select a customer, SKU, quantity, currency, and evaluation time. Compare the live and proposed unit prices and check which threshold applies. Staff with publishing permission can publish immediately or schedule a future activation. Use version history to cancel a pending version or restore an earlier published configuration.

Upload a UTF-8 CSV with the heading `sku,min_quantity,price`. Select **Validate CSV** and review any row errors. Apply the validated file to the draft and publish when ready. Export the draft for a copy of its SKU prices and thresholds. Download the file within 24 hours.

## Restrictions and decisions

Stores with contract pricing enabled can match a list to a company,
location, or storefront channel. Company membership comes from the customer's
assigned location. Use **Pricing contracts** for customer assignments and
restricted products, then edit the list’s draft and use **Preview as
customer** to check the matching channel.

For vendor SKUs, request approval of the prices, floor, stacking policy,
and funding owner before publishing. Vendors review the exact terms in
their workspace. A store-funded contribution is recorded separately from
the customer's payment and adjusted proportionally when merchandise is
refunded.

The server recalculates cart prices. Existing orders keep their original amounts. Price changes require the relevant permission and store workspace.

Validity dates include both the start and end date in the store’s timezone. A list awaiting audience review does not apply to shoppers until staff confirm its audience. To change a populated list’s currency, create a new list with amounts entered in that currency.

## Quantity and pricing authority

Sign in and select the product option and quantity to see the unit price you qualify for. Product details show upcoming eligible quantity prices. Product cards check the price for one unit. The cart can apply a different price if the quantity changes or an offer expires. Catalog price filters and sorting use public catalog prices.

If the storefront displays an estimated conversion, the cart shows the payable currency and amount. The server applies eligible cart discounts, tax, and shipping when it calculates the order total. If the price changes after you review it, review the refreshed total before placing the order.

Authorized store staff can inspect pricing within their own catalog. Vendor pricing access is limited to the vendor and the selected store workspace.

## Data flow and outcome

Variant price + eligible list + applicable discount rules → cart recalculation → checkout money snapshot.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
