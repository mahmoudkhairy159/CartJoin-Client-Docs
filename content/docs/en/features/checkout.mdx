## How to use it

Follow Checkout’s four steps: Address, Delivery, Payment, Review. Confirm the vendor split and final total, then submit once and wait for the invoice confirmation. Guests use cash on delivery and keep the private confirmation/PDF. Signed-in customers can also continue from Account → Invoices. A phone is required; email is optional.

If your selected method is unavailable at review, choose delivery again. A temporary quote outage leaves the cart intact. Retry checkout once quotes are available. The chosen method determines the charge even when you request a later day.

After checkout, open the invoice to find the payment action and all seller orders. Each seller order has its own fulfillment progress and may travel in multiple parcels.

On a supported device, signed-in shoppers at an enrolled store can select delivery and choose **Review order with Apple Pay**. Check the address and total before placing the order and completing payment on Paymob.

### Open a draft the store shared

Sign in with the account the store selected. Open the draft link, enter your delivery address, choose the shipping and payment methods, and review the current total before confirming. The draft uses its own item list. Your regular basket stays available for a separate purchase.

If the quote changes, check the updated total before confirming again. If the draft already became an invoice, the link opens the existing purchase. If the link expired or the store withdrew it, ask the store for a new draft.

When a merchant requests an optional reminder while preparing a draft, the selected buyer can receive one notice in the store-account inbox during the final six hours of the draft. Open it in the account the store selected. Drafts that expire, are withdrawn, or become orders suppress an unsent reminder. Review current availability and prices before confirming the draft.

In **Order drafts**, merchants can compare created, placed and paid drafts and see how many reminders were requested, sent or suppressed. The last 30 days use the date each draft was created. A later outcome counts if that draft is still in the window. An empty window shows zero counts.

## Restrictions and decisions

Empty or invalid carts cannot check out. Address, stock, shipping and payment method must be valid for the tenant. Idempotency prevents a replay of the same command from creating a second purchase. Group totals must sum to the original total to the cent.

## Address and delivery selection

Choose the country first, then its state and city. Saved addresses resolve their location names before delivery options are requested. Select an offered shipping method and review its cost and estimated days; an empty method list needs an address or coverage check. Payment methods follow the store’s configured order. The final Review step remains necessary before placing the purchase.

## Data flow and outcome

Validate cart → lock and group lines → reserve stock → snapshot orders → create a draft invoice without a legal number → clear cart in the transaction → initiate payment after placement. The draft is issued with its number at the first successful payment, at a zero-total checkout, or at manual approval for COD and bank transfer. A payment redirect alone does not prove success: after the gateway you return to a payment-return page that refreshes the status automatically. If checkout created the invoice but payment could not start, use **Continue to payment** from confirmation or from the invoice in your account.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
