## How to use it

Open Invoices and inspect the document, vendor orders, totals and payment status. Customers use Account → Invoices and the invoice detail. Operators use Invoice sequences / integrity where authorized to review numbering anomalies.

<SystemTour tour="feature-invoices" compact />

Open an issued credit note from its refund in **Account → Payments**, or browse the **Credit notes** section. The credit note links back to the owning invoice. A refund can already be visible while its note is being issued.

The invoice title follows its recorded state. An issued invoice awaiting payment shows **Unpaid invoice**. A partly paid invoice shows **Partially paid invoice**. The original number remains the purchase reference. A stored PDF reflects the state recorded when the file was generated.

## Restrictions and decisions

Invoice numbering is tenant-specific and must not be manually renumbered. Vendors see only their allowed line economics with buyer information redacted in vendor invoice resources. Cancellation of one child order does not mean all sibling orders are cancelled.

In **Invoice sequences**, review the audit timestamp in UTC before acting on an exception. The scheduled audit reports the affected ranges and opens the exact recorded document when one exists. Cancelled numbered invoices remain in their series. Older numbers that cannot be identified with confidence appear for review. A request to change the numbering reset shows its effective UTC date. It applies to future issuance, and the original issuer snapshot stays with each issued document.

## Read the vendor and purchased items

The customer invoice can show each linked order’s vendor and purchased items, including SKU, variant, quantity, unit price, discount and line total. Pictures follow the saved purchase snapshot; a missing older picture does not change the financial record. Distinguish a per-unit price from the full quantity-adjusted line total.

## Data flow and outcome

Checkout → issued invoice → payment success marks paid → settled refund produces credit note. When every child order cancels, the invoice can be cancelled by the lifecycle integration.

## When something goes wrong

Check workspace, permission, record state and field errors first. If an action times out, inspect the record and its history before retrying. Escalate with the screen, record identifier and time; omit passwords and payment secrets.
