## Updating captures

Refresh captures when the corresponding navigation or behavior changes. Use demo data. Exclude passwords, tokens, bank details, redeemable gift-card codes, private payment proofs and real customer personal data. A screenshot proves the visible screen on its capture date; it does not prove every underlying mutation works.
